# Brag Plan: Object Department Goods

## What is this app?
A compact demonstration storefront for useful home, lifestyle, stationery, and tech objects.

## The angle
Treat the shopping path like a tactile independent shop journal: browse with intention, pause on one object, then see the bag and honest demo checkout.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
“Useful things, chosen with care.” lands over a warm paper field and a composed still life.

## Key moments (the middle)
- A three-object catalog with real categories from the project.
- A ceramic detail view with its actual “Add to bag” action.
- The bag shows one sample item and clearly labels the order as a demo.

## Outro / punchline
“Good things, kept close.” Object Department Goods.

## User flow worth showing
Open catalog → inspect an object → add it to the bag and see the demo checkout.

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: A warm editorial product film, like turning through a small independent shop journal.
- Interpretation: Gentle pace, paper texture, serif-led typography, and precise object details.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 20 seconds

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: warm paper `#f2eee5` (adapted from the Goods interface)
- Accent: rust `#a7553d`
- Text: espresso `#29251f`
- Display font: Georgia serif (local fallback)
- Body font: Arial sans-serif
- Strongest visual element: Object-led product cards and the persistent bag flow.

## Share copy (draft)
Object Department Goods: a small demonstration shop for useful everyday objects. Browse the collection, inspect the details, and build a bag.

## Audio direction
- Role: warm, light instrumental bed.
- Music: Happy Beats / Business Moves Vol. 1, Sascha Ende (CC BY 4.0).
- Music treatment: restrained bed, low in the mix, trimmed to 20 seconds and mixed quietly.
- Music cue guidance: bundled cue preset; clean reveal near 17.02s. Catalog elements stagger on the half-second beat grid.
- Audio-reactive treatment: none; extraction support unavailable in this local setup.
- SFX posture: sparse; no simulated checkout sound.
- Audio-coupled moments: still-life reveal; catalog arrival; closing lockup.
- Restraint rule: no sales claims, live payment, shipping, or invented product promises.

## Storyboard
### Scene 1 — Shop journal — 3s
The title and a simple tabletop arrangement establish the shop. Sequential/interaction: title then three drawn objects. Audio intent: soft opening. Audio-coupled idea: title settles on a music cue.
### Scene 2 — Catalog — 5s
Three representative objects and the project’s real categories. Sequential/interaction: cards enter one by one. Audio intent: quiet rhythmic movement. Audio-coupled idea: stagger cards to the beat grid.
### Scene 3 — Product detail — 4.5s
Sunday ceramic detail and ADD TO BAG. Sequential/interaction: detail panel slides into place. Audio intent: tactile accent. Audio-coupled idea: button settles at a cue.
### Scene 4 — Bag — 4.5s
A single clearly fictional sample item; demo-order wording stays visible. Sequential/interaction: item and total reveal. Audio intent: warm confirmation without an implied payment. Audio-coupled idea: total appears on beat.
### Scene 5 — Close — 3s
Object Department Goods lockup and “Good things, kept close.” Sequential/interaction: none. Intentional reading hold is annotated in the composition. Audio intent: soft finish. Audio-coupled idea: music fades under the mark.
