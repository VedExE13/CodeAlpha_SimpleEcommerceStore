# Object Department Goods video

A 20-second landscape product film made for this independent CodeAlpha submission.

- [Watch the video](brag.mp4)
- [Poster image](brag.jpg)
- [Storyboard and creative plan](brag-plan.md)
- [Composition source](composition/index.html)
- [Share caption](share-copy.txt)

The screens are staged illustrations of the project's UI. Any people, tasks, items, and messages shown are fictional sample content.

## Re-render locally

Requires Node.js 22+, FFmpeg, and HyperFrames.

    npx hyperframes render composition --output brag.mp4 --quality standard
